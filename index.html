About Us

Nichido Solutions Inc. is a private research and development company based in Kyoto, Japan.

Our work focuses on identifying inefficiencies in the foundational systems that shape modern infrastructure. We conduct research in several key domains including communication systems, measurement standards, and global commerce. In many of these areas, existing paradigms were not designed for the technological and economic demands of the future. Our goal is to explore how these systems could be redesigned and improved to create lasting value for industry and society.

Our Research:

Rethinking Writing:

Systems for Clearer Thought

In an age of information overload, how can we improve our most fundamental tool for thinking? We are engineering new writing systems designed from the ground up to enhance human cognition, enabling more efficient reading, intuitive writing, and ultimately, more powerful structured thought.

Rethinking Measurement:

Redefining Fit for Global Commerce

How can we solve the universal problem of inconsistent sizing that plagues online retail? Our research focuses on creating new universal standards for physical goods. We are developing intelligent frameworks that allows for perfect size translation across brands and regions, eliminating uncertainty for shoppers and costly inefficiencies for businesses.

Direct Commerce:

A New Architecture for Global Trade

What would a truly borderless marketplace look like? We are architecting next-generation frameworks for cross-border e-commerce. This model is designed to empower international shoppers and sellers to connect and transact directly, removing unnecessary intermediaries to create a more transparent and efficient global market.
